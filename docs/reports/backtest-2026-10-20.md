# Backtest report (2026-10-20)

> Historical simulation, not a promise. Results hold only under the assumptions below.

## Data coverage

- Window: 2025-04-20 → 2026-10-19; stable Pendle markets with history: 289.
- 24 of 289 stable markets are whitelisted (tradable in the backtest)
- Parking series: 6 pools (DefiLlama daily `apyBase`).

## Assumptions

- Gas: ethereum 3 gwei + L1 0 USD, arbitrum 0.02 gwei + L1 0.02 USD, base 0.01 gwei + L1 0.02 USD (no free hosted L2/L1 gas history; Ethereum set conservatively high).
- ETH price from DefiLlama daily chart (fallback 3000 USD).
- Stablecoins assumed at peg while held; incident-catalog expected losses subtracted afterwards.
- Protocol TVL / peg screens assumed passed historically (data not available point-in-time).
- PT price derived from hourly implied APY: (1 + APY) ** (-days / 365).
- Slippage from the pool-TVL heuristic (no historical quotes exist).
- Decision step: 1 day, 0:00:00.

## Method

- The live decision code (NLY, risk rules, allocator, paper portfolio) replays history daily.
- Tier premiums are chosen on the first 60% of the window (tuning) by incident-adjusted APY; the remaining 40% (validation) is never used for tuning.
- Benchmark targets use the validation window with the agreed default premiums (A 2 pp, B 6 pp).
